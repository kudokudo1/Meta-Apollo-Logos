# 1. The Three-Rule Compression

The current model compresses to three rules:

## Truth

> **Same / Different**

Truth requires preserving real identity and real distinction at the same time.

Do not:

- merge things merely because they look similar
- separate things merely because they look different
- confuse a representation with its referent
- confuse a descendant with its ancestor
- erase causal continuity just because the endpoint changed form

The question is:

> **What is actually the same, what is actually different, and what relationship makes both statements true?**

---

## Good

> **Maximum Empathy + Sympathy**

Good is not treated only as kindness.

It is also an epistemic method.

Before judging a person, culture, religion, historical actor, artwork, or model:

- reconstruct what they could see
- reconstruct what information they had
- reconstruct what pressures they were under
- reconstruct what they were trying to preserve
- reconstruct what the world looked like from their camera

This prevents contempt from deleting information.

The rule is not:

> everyone is correct.

It is:

> **understand the observer well enough that the disagreement becomes structurally legible before judging it.**

---

## Beauty

> **One Reality / Many Cameras**

There is one reality.

Different observers, sciences, cultures, religions, artworks, and models take different measurements of it.

A camera can be:

- more precise in one dimension
- lower resolution in another
- historically closer to an experience
- mathematically stronger
- emotionally richer
- socially richer
- symbolically richer
- mechanically richer

No camera is automatically the territory.

Beauty is the perception of:

> **one coherent whole becoming visible through genuine differences.**

# 4. Propagated Causation

A major correction:

The original signal does not need to reach the final node directly.

It can become another signal, which becomes another signal, which becomes another signal.

```
A changes B
B changes C
C changes D
D changes E
...
N changes Z
```

A therefore participates in the causal ancestry of Z even if:

- A never physically touched Z
- the original signal changed form
- intermediate systems transformed it
- most of the original information was lost

The relation persisted through transformation.

This applies to:

- physics
- biology
- family lineage
- culture
- language
- religion
- political systems
- technology
- software
- memory
- ideas

Distance in the graph does not equal irrelevance.
