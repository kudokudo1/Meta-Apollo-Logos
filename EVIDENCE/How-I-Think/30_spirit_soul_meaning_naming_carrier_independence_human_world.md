# 4. Spirit: The Current Definition

The previous formulation was still too weak because it reduced spirit to "information" or "pattern."

The stronger current claim is:

> **A spirit is a real, supernatural meaning-identity that exists through human consciousness, can be instantiated in one or many people, can move between people through representations, and can organize the people who carry it.**

Important distinction:

> **carrier-independent does not mean human-independent.**

The spirit is not identical to:

- one body
- one voice
- one recording
- one sentence
- one artwork
- one building
- one brain state
- one particular physical carrier

But in the user's current model, it **does depend on at least one human mind carrying the concept/meaning**.

Therefore:

```
NO HUMAN HOLDS X
→ spirit X does not presently exist

ONE HUMAN HOLDS X
→ spirit X exists
→ one person can embody / be possessed by X

MANY HUMANS HOLD X
→ X can exist through many people simultaneously
→ distributed spirit / culture / nation / movement / tradition
```

This is why a spirit can seem "everywhere and nowhere."

It is not located like a chair.

It can be present through many carriers at once.
