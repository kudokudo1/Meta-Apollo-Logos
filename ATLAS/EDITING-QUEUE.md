# Writing rules discovered during editing

## Remove unnecessary “we”

Let the thing being examined become the grammatical subject when possible.

## Do not make ideas compete unnecessarily

No “winner.”

No “we defeated X.”

No “we proved Y.”

Describe what something reveals and why it remains useful.

## Do not tell the reader what they are supposed to think

Especially with historical, religious, national, technological, and cultural references.

Put the thing there.

Let the reader see it.

## Same thing, different thing

Vary the representation without losing the underlying structure.

## Weird but coherent

Do not normalize the writing until it becomes generic.

Clean it.

Do not sterilize it.

## The author should be present without constantly announcing himself

The text can speak about the thing itself without repeatedly creating a team or group called “we.”

## The light should remain continuous

The subject can change.

The underlying transmission should not disappear.

## The document is not finished because the document ends

The document is another carrier in a longer process.
