## WHY THIS MAP EXISTS

A Hospital worker should not need to inherit the entire history of Meta Apollo Logos // Post-Apollo in order to do one bounded job. A **knowledge Family** is a small neighborhood of repositories and/or subsystems connected by useful relationships, shared interfaces, or documented dependencies. It provides retrieval routes, **not copied source documents**.

Suggested context selection: **UNIVERSAL → relevant FAMILY → PATIENT → ROOM → precise EVIDENCE**, skipping unnecessary depths. These are *information scopes*, not five mandatory intake files.

**Knowledge Family ≠ Hospital Team ≠ Floor ≠ Patient ≠ Room ≠ Bed/branch.** Membership neither grants authority nor enrolls a repository in a Hospital Floor. The existing `.meta-apollo.yml` field `family: Meta Apollo Logos` identifies the umbrella project family; it is not this narrower knowledge grouping.

The source of truth for a technical contract, implementation, report, or historical artifact remains in its owning repository/Hospital record. Public `FAMILY` links in READMEs are navigation; they are not dependency evidence or approvals for these narrower memberships.

## STATUS AND RELATIONSHIP VOCABULARY

- **MAPPED:** specific relationship and intended membership confirmed from evidence **and accepted through the authorized promotion path**. No initial row below claims this state.
- **PROVISIONAL:** discovered and provisionally classified; candidate memberships need a decision or further relationship verification.
- **UNRESOLVED:** discovered, but meaningful classification or relationship is not yet supported. Keep listed rather than inventing membership.
- **HISTORICAL:** archive/provenance node; useful for understanding the past, *not* current implementation authority.

Each record also has a **kind**: `PROJECT`, `ARCHIVE`, or `DIRECTORY`. Kind is independent of promotion status. A future subsystem record may use kind `SUBSYSTEM`.

Relationship labels must distinguish **runtime/configuration dependency**, **shared technical contract**, **conceptual/design affiliation**, and **historical evidence**. A link, common theme, or shared umbrella branding does not imply runtime coupling. Do not convert candidate relationships into verified edges by association alone.

## MAINTENANCE // PROVENANCE, OWNERSHIP, PROMOTION

**Registry purpose:** discover where to look. **Patient authority:** owns actual contracts, implementation, source files, and local runtime facts. **Hospital:** owns accepted Room/Assignment/Chart/report coordination and interpretation. **How I Think:** owns collaboration guidance. **PX/Git:** provide relevant operational facts/evidence within their existing scopes.

A proposed change should record:

1. **Subject:** exact repository or subsystem identity, and proposed primary/secondary knowledge Family memberships.
2. **Relation kind:** runtime/configuration, shared contract, conceptual, or historical; specify which part is established and what remains uncertain.
3. **Source and as-of:** stable document path, branch/commit when material, and verification level (directly inspected, independently reviewed, operator-accepted, or unresolved).
4. **Affected neighbor:** other repository/subsystem/Family touched; do not assume permission from a cross-link.
5. **Decision/provenance:** proposal author, validator, authorized acceptance, and a pointer to the superseded record when revised.

**Process:** report or inspection → candidate mapping → source validation (e.g., read-only Intern F) → affected Patient/authority review where consequential → operator or explicitly delegated acceptance → registry promotion. Nurse 4 may prepare a bounded edit; Head Nurse coordinates review/escalation; Intern F is read-only unless separately reassigned. Neither source inspection nor report submission automatically promotes a record.

For an accepted change, update the entry and record what prior classification it supersedes through Git history and an explicit note when that relationship is not obvious. Preserve open questions and rejected classifications as cited historical evidence rather than silently deleting the reasoning.

**Update triggers:** new/renamed repo, new verified cross-repo contract, affected Patient's accepted architecture change, source-link drift, or a report revealing a genuinely relevant boundary. Routine commits in unrelated parts of a repository do not force this map to reload or change.

**Do not** create an authoritative live Hospital `FAMILY` Chart scope here: the current [PX Hospital store](https://github.com/kudokudo1/The-Post-Apollo-Dev-Exp/blob/main/bin/px-hospital-store) and [Taskbars Chart service](https://github.com/kudokudo1/The-Post-Apollo-Project/blob/main/services/hospital/HospitalChartService.qml) currently recognize `PATIENT` and `ROOM`. Schema/API/UI migration, automated report ingestion, Floor assignment, and automatic canonical promotion are separate authorization and verification work.
